# ORBIT LAW — Concept

## Brand

**ORBIT LAW** — modern legal consultancy. Serious, premium, trustworthy editorial presence. Not a corporate B2B checklist site (that is NORTH & CO) and not pastry luxury (NOIR).

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

Founders, operators, and families needing counsel on corporate, dispute, and privacy matters. Ages ~30–60. Value clarity, discretion, and calm authority.

## Business goal

Drive **Speak with counsel**. Secondary: practice areas, team trust, insights.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Ink navy parchment — editorial law chambers, quiet authority |
| vs NORTH & CO | Not light paper forest cards — darker editorial, large type |
| vs NOIR | Not champagne pastry — slate + parchment, not gold desserts |
| Avoid | Apex red, Volt lime, Mona ivory-bronze clinic, purple AI |

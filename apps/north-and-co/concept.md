# NORTH & CO — Concept

## Brand

**NORTH & CO** — business consulting / strategy studio. Clear frameworks, measurable engagements, corporate calm. Classic commercial B2B site — not architecture, not experimental art direction (that is Frame / House).

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

Founders and operators 30–55 hiring strategy support for growth, ops, and positioning. Want structure, cases, and a short path to a call.

## Business goal

Drive **Start an engagement**. Secondary: services, cases, process, trust.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Cool paper / ink corporate — structured, quiet confidence |
| vs Frame | Not concrete geometry / iron oxide / scrub |
| vs Northline | Not navy RE filters — lighter paper field + forest ink accent |
| Avoid | Apex racing red, Volt lime, Vanta teal, purple AI, cream+terracotta |

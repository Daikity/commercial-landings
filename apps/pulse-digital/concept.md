# PULSE DIGITAL — Concept

## Brand

**PULSE DIGITAL** — digital agency. The most tech-forward landing in the monorepo: scroll storytelling, horizontal work, sticky process, magnetic CTA. Experimental motion without losing usability.

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

Product founders and marketing leads 28–45 who want a sharp digital partner for sites, products, and campaigns.

## Business goal

Drive **Start a build**. Secondary: work proof, process clarity, capabilities.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Void black / electric cyan signal — tech agency pulse |
| vs APEX | Not racing red dyno grit |
| vs VOLT | Not EV lime editorial calm |
| vs FRAME | Not concrete iron architecture |
| Avoid | Purple AI glow, cream+terracotta, Orbit parchment |

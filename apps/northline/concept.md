# NORTHLINE — Concept

## Brand

**NORTHLINE** — modern residential real estate agency. Clear listings, calm confidence, city-north clarity. Classic commercial site a buyer would filter and book a viewing from.

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

Buyers and renters 28–50 looking for apartments, houses, and lofts in northern metro corridors. Want filters, honest cards, and a fast viewing request.

## Business goal

Drive **Book a viewing**. Secondary: show featured stock, services, locations, trust.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Cool steel / navy agency — crisp, map-minded, not spa |
| Photography | Architecture exteriors, bright interiors, skyline — not bakery/wellness/clinic |
| Avoid | Aura eucalyptus; Kairo mist sage; Mona ivory bronze; Ferment grit; purple AI; cream+terracotta |

## Typography

- Display: Archivo (agency condensed clarity)
- Body: Public Sans (UI-readable for filters/cards)

## Layout (Classic Commercial)

Hero → Featured properties (filters + cards) → Services → Locations → Why us → Testimonials → CTA / viewing form → Contacts

## Motion (Level A)

Soft reveal, card hover image scale, filter transitions. No sticky hijack.

## CTA / Form

**Book a viewing** — name, email, property interest, note. Mock submit.

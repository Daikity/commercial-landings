# VOLT GARAGE — Concept

## Brand

**VOLT GARAGE** — EV / premium automotive studio. Quiet technical luxury: charging calibration, software updates, and finish care for electric vehicles. Not a classic detailing shop — a voltage atelier.

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

EV owners and lease fleets, 30–55. Want editorial calm, clear studio capabilities, and a reserved bay — not a price-list garage.

## Business goal

Drive **Reserve a bay**. Secondary: show vehicles, technology, studio atmosphere.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Dark EV showroom — cool voltage, minimal chrome, light streaks |
| vs VANTA | Not ceramic teal graphite detailing cards — editorial whitespace + horizontal vehicles |
| vs NOIR | Not champagne gold pastry — technical lime + mono labels |
| Avoid | Purple AI, cream+terracotta, Vanta teal, Frame iron oxide |

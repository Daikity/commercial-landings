# MONA Skin — Concept

## Brand

**MONA Skin** — premium skincare clinic. Clean, minimal, medical-luxury. Editorial calm, not spa fluff.

Fictional brand for portfolio demonstration.

## Target audience

Adults 28–50 seeking clinical-grade skincare consultations and treatments. Value expertise, quiet luxury, evidence-led care.

## Business goal

Drive **Request a consult**. Secondary: communicate philosophy, treatments, ingredients, expert trust.

## Visual direction (anti-clone)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Mood | Cool ivory clinical luxury, editorial whitespace |
| Photography | Marble, bottles, skin macro, clinic interiors, premium portraits |
| Avoid | Aura eucalyptus commercial; Noir black pastry; Maison bakery warm; purple AI; cream+terracotta cliché |

## Layout (Premium Editorial)

Hero · Philosophy · Treatments · Ingredients · Experts · Results · Testimonials · Booking · Footer

Asymmetric editorial grids, large type, restrained motion (reveals + light parallax).

## Motion (Level B)

Soft image reveals, typography fades, subtle section parallax. No sticky hijack.
